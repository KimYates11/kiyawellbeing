Yin & Violin

Rest·Resonance·Release
An evening to pause, reconnect and restore — through stillness & music. Guided by Kim Yates · Live violin by Jessica Leigh

Immerse yourself in a magical candlelit evening where restorative Yin Yoga meets the soulful resonance of live violin, creating space to slow down, soften & surrender as we move into the festive season.

As the music flows through each pose, allow yourself to be carried into deep relaxation, closing with the soothing vibrations of singing bowls, gentle chimes and a blissful Savasana.
